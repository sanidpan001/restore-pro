Frequently Asked Questions

How does AI old photo restoration work?
RestorePro analyzes your old photo directly in your browser and applies a series of intelligent image-processing steps: it increases contrast, removes noise and scratches, sharpens details, and simulates natural colorization for black & white images. Everything runs client-side, so your photos never leave your device.
Is RestorePro really free with no watermark?
Yes. RestorePro is completely free to use with no sign-in, no subscription, and no watermark on your restored photos. There are no hidden fees or download limits — restore as many photos as you like.
Can I colorize black & white photos?
Absolutely. The "Colorize B&W" option applies a warm, natural color simulation to grayscale images, giving faded black & white photos a lifelike sepia-to-color tone. You can toggle it on or off before restoring, and combine it with scratch removal and face enhancement for the best results.
Are my photos uploaded to a server?
No. RestorePro processes 100% of your photos locally in your browser using the Canvas API. Your images are never uploaded to any server, which keeps your memories private and makes restoration instant.
